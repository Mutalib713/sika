## Colour

Never hand-picked. Generated by `palette.py --world "<...>"` or `--seed "#hex"`,
or pinned from `tools/palette-picker.html`. A pinned palette is canon and does
not get "improved" mid-project.

- [ ] Every image has alt text or is marked decorative
